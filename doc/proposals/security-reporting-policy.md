# Proposed security reporting policy for the Whive fork

This is a **draft**, not a verified reporting service. Reviewed repository base: `889dcb01e69cdc8365da88c2f79cc9eb9254ae18`.

The inherited security policy names upstream contacts or programs. Those may remain appropriate for upstream issues, but do not establish responsibility for Whive-specific modifications. Preserve the original policy until maintainers verify and adopt a replacement; do not imply upstream endorsement of this fork.

The shared proposed process is `security-reporting-policy-draft.md` in the [Whive WIP repository](https://github.com/whiveio/wips). That package is under local preparation; publication and adoption are not implied.

## Proposed adoption requirements

Verify willing maintainers, supported versions, private intake and an independently verifiable contact route. Public project contact candidates are [@whiveio](https://x.com/whiveio) and `protocol@whive.io`; current monitoring and confidential handling are **unverified**. Do not send sensitive details until the intended recipients and secure method are confirmed. Agents draft coordination; authorized humans send it.

Define capacity-backed acknowledgment/triage, patch review, recovery guidance and coordinated disclosure. Avoid publishing exploit-ready reproductions in public issues or proposals before remediation coordination. Never ask for seeds, private keys or credentials. A public repository's non-deployed docs are still public.

The historical email source is the [Whive protocol yellow paper](https://github.com/whiveio/whive/blob/889dcb01e69cdc8365da88c2f79cc9eb9254ae18/WHIVE_PROTOCOL_YELLOW_PAPER.md#L11), inspected at the pinned source revision. This establishes publication of the identifier, not current mailbox monitoring or confidential intake.

## Evidence required before adoption

Record contact provenance/control confirmation, responsible maintainers, supported-version scope, secure-channel verification and a report-to-release rehearsal. Keep confidential records outside public commits. Until then, the proposed policy remains a draft; no response-time or support guarantee is made.
